## 1. Main Players (the platforms themselves)

### Polymarket
- Founded 2020; HQ Manhattan, NYC (Source: Wikipedia/Britannica, 2026)
- Raised ~$2.3B across 7 rounds (Source: Tracxn, 2026); in talks for $400M at $15B valuation (Source: Bloomberg, April 2026)

- Business model: on-chain (Polygon + UMA oracle); CFTC-regulated in US via QCX acquisition November 2025 (Source: CoinDesk, Nov 2025)
- Differentiator: largest on-chain prediction market; ~60% of volume is non-sports (Source: next.io/alphascope, 2026)

### Kalshi
- Founded 2018; HQ NYC; launched July 2021 (Source: Wikipedia/Contrary Research, 2026)
- Raised >$1B at $22B valuation March 2026, led by Coatue (Source: Bloomberg, March 2026)
- Prior: $1B Series E at $11B Dec 2025; $300M Series D at $5B Oct 2025 (Source: Kalshi News/Brave New Coin, 2025)
- Business model: CFTC-regulated DCM (first federally regulated US prediction exchange) (Source: CFTC/Kalshi Help, 2026)
- Differentiator: controls ~89% of US regulated prediction market volume (Source: CoinDesk, April 2026)

### PredictIt

- Funding raised: no reliable source

- Annualized revenue run rate: ~$300–338M (Source: Yahoo/AInvest, April 2026)
- **Back-of-envelope LTV:** inputs are mean-skewed by whales (2% of traders earn >$1K profit). Median-user LTV not constructable from public data.

### Kalshi — constructable with caveats
- Volume: $13.07B April 2026; 88.4M transactions (Source: Artemis, April 2026)
- **Avg transaction: ~$148** (derived: $13.07B / 88.4M)
- **Transactions per MAU: ~17/month** (derived: 88.4M / 5.1M)
- 2025 take rate: ~1.15% of volume ($263.5M / $22.88B) (Source: Yahoo/CDC Gaming, Feb 2026)
- **Derived monthly revenue/user: ~$29** ($148 × 17 × 0.0115)
- **Derived LTV at 6mo tenure: ~$174; at 12mo: ~$348** (heavy caveat: sports-weighted, cohort-skewed, tenure unknown)
