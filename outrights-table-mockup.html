Why this layout reuses for leagues

One component, one column contract: Team · Verdict · [stat columns] · Model · Market · Edge. The stat block is data-driven — for a tournament-winner market before kick-off it renders —; for a league it fills with P W D L GF GA GD Pts straight from the standings feed. No structural change, just a different data source.

Team leads as the pinned identity column with Verdict right beside it. Rows sort by Model probability on winner markets and by Points on league tables, and each row links to /outrights/{team} for the full case. On mobile the table collapses to Team · Verdict · P · Pts · Model — the things a phone reader actually scans.
