# Odds Primer — Presenter Bible (v1: "Modern presenter")

The locked on-camera host. Use the same description on every future episode so she stays recognisable across hundreds of videos. Keep a seed image: generate her once at high quality, save the best frame, and feed it back as a reference image in any tool that supports one (Veo, Runway, Midjourney `--cref`, HeyGen avatar).

## Identity (do not change between episodes)

- Female, late 20s–early 30s, naturally striking, not over-styled.
- Dark brown hair with subtle balayage, soft waves, just past the shoulder.
- Warm brown eyes, defined but natural brows, light natural makeup, subtle freckles.
- Smart-casual: fine-knit top in cream or charcoal under a tailored navy blazer; minimal jewellery (small studs).
- Relaxed, confident broadcaster energy — calm authority, not "presenter smile".

## Studio (consistent set)

Modern editorial sports newsroom. Soft key light from front-left, gentle rim light. Background: deep navy with a large blurred broadcast screen behind her reading **ARGENTINA / WORLD CUP 2026** in white + soft blue. Palette: dark navy, white, soft blue. No neon, no betting graphics, no casino styling.
