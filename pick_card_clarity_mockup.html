What changed

  1. Called-side chip next to the Pick label — "Argentina to win". This is the one element the current card is missing.
  2. Austria dims back in the matchup title, so the eye lands on the picked side first.
  3. "Argentina to win" stat line anchors the 76% / 57% / +18.9pp, so the numbers obviously belong to the pick — not the matchup in general.

Only fires on Picks, which carry a called side. Pass and Avoid have no side and keep their current treatment. Voice stays anti-hype — "Argentina to win", never "back Argentina".
