How to read this

Each tile is the same lockup geometry — only the wordmark text varies. The reticle proportions (28px circle, 4px crosshair extension, central dot) are constant. Names with a "THE" article (Scout Room, Pitch Line, Stand, Morning XI/11) gain editorial weight from the lighter prefix; the brand reads as a publication, not a product. Names without an article (Scout Room solo, Odds Primer, Scoutline) feel a notch closer to "tool" or "feature" — slightly weaker as a publication wordmark in this system.

What to look for. Which lockup feels inevitable on second viewing? Which one would you comfortably put on a printed match-day programme? Which one wears the inverted (white-on-navy) variant best — masthead, social cards, and merch all need that mode to work too.

Provisional reads. The Scout Room + The Morning XI have the strongest fit with the system as drawn. The Stand is the cleanest single-syllable option — strongest inverted-on-navy presence. Scoutline and Odds Primer read less like a publication and more like a feature; if either of those wins on positioning, worth re-testing the lockup with a different glyph (the reticle may not be the right system glyph for those names).
