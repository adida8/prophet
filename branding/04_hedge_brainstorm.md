# Hedge Brainstorm — Football-Leaning Name Candidates

Backup pool in case the three finalists don't excite once seen in real clothes. All names mash football-native vocabulary with an implicit clarity / observation angle. Domain availability checked via DNS NS records (May 2026).

**Reading the table.**
- ✅ = appears available (no NS records)
- 🅿️ = parked at registrar (often acquirable cheap, sometimes not)
- ❌ = registered and in active use

---

## Tier A — strong candidates worth a real look

### The Boot Room
Liverpool's iconic tactical/coaching room where Shankly, Paisley, Fagan, and Dalglish met to plan matches. Carries deep football lore + tactical wisdom + insider explanation. Voice writes itself: "from the boot room," "boot-room briefing." Unique among options for having a *story* baked in.

| Domain | Status |
|---|---|
| bootroom.com | ❌ |
| thebootroom.com | ❌ |
| bootroom.football | ✅ |
| thebootroom.football | ✅ |
| thebootroomfc.com | ✅ |

**Verdict:** Strong concept. Domain is workable on `.football`. Liverpool association could be a feature (rich heritage) or a constraint (perceived as Liverpool-specific).

## Names ruled out

- **Onside** — `.com`, `.football`, `theonside.com` and `onsidefc.com` all taken. No clean path.
- **Kickoff** — `.com`, `.football`, `thekickoff.com` all taken.
- **First Touch** — domains heavily contested across all viable TLDs.

---

## My recommendation if pivoting from finalists

If *Read the Game* doesn't land when you see it in real clothes, the strongest hedges in priority order would be:

1. **The Boot Room** — strongest concept, real heritage, workable domain.
2. **Set Piece** — modern, crisp, clean availability, scales.
3. **The Pundit** — most direct mash of football + clarity.
4. **The Counter** — sharpest editorial positioning.

All four would survive the WC, work year-round, and don't carry the brand-collision risk of *The Touchline*.
