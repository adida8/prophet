# Prophet — Claude Design prompt for brand mock exploration

**Authored 2026-05-02 after the brand-landscape research session. Locked palette direction: white paper + dark navy ink + flame-orange accent. Modern sans throughout, no broadsheet serif. Reticle/scope motif retained as system glyph.**

Copy the block below into Claude Design (or any LLM-driven design tool) and iterate.

---

**Brand context.** Prophet is a football oddschecker that explains betting markets to fans rather than tipping bets. Editorial register, anti-tipster, anti-gambling-promo. Each piece of content is framed as a "scout report" on a fixture or market — observational, evaluative, premium publication feel. The logo concept uses a reticle/scope motif. Launch is the 2026 World Cup; the working name is *The Scout* (also test as *Read the Game* and *Prophet*).

**Palette (locked).**

- Paper: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink / deep navy (primary): `#0E2240`
- Flame orange (single accent): `#FF5A1F`
- Graphite (secondary text/structure): `#3A3F47`
- Warm rule / soft block: `#F0ECE2`

The orange is the flare, not the field — sparingly used for one CTA, the reticle dot, and one piece of data emphasis per composition. No other accent colours.

**Typography.** Modern sans throughout — pick one family and commit (Inter, Söhne, or Helvetica Now). Heavy condensed weight for headlines, neo-grotesque for body, monospaced for data rows (JetBrains Mono / IBM Plex Mono). No broadsheet serif. No script.

**Reticle motif.** A circle with crosshairs through it; a small flame-orange dot at the centre. Reads as scope/scout sight. System glyph, not the wordmark itself.

**Voice samples** (drop into mocks where copy is needed):

- Eyebrow stamp: `SCOUT REPORT · GROUP F`
- Headline: `Brazil's path to the final, scouted.`
- Subhead: "Three things the markets are missing about Tite's side, what to back, what to fade, and where the value sits in the futures."
- Data row (monospaced): `FIX 14 · BRA v ARG · 21:00 · CALL Argentina +130 · CONF medium · EDGE +4.2%`
- Section labels: `THE READ`, `THE CALL`, `THE FADE`, `MATCHDAY BRIEF`

**Produce four compositions, 1280-wide web or A4 portrait:**

1. **Homepage hero.** Navy masthead bar with reticle + wordmark left, small-caps date right. Below: a scout-report hero — eyebrow stamp, big condensed headline, two-line lede, monospaced FIX/CALL/CONF/EDGE row. Rule line. Three content cards in a 3-up grid below (each: eyebrow tag, headline, 2-line lede, monospaced footer with section + read time).

2. **Single scout report page.** Article view. Big headline, lede, byline, single-column body, one flame-orange pull-quote block. Right rail: a "THE CALL" stamped box with market price, edge, confidence.

3. **Logo lockups.** Reticle + wordmark in (a) horizontal navy-on-white, (b) inverted white-on-navy, (c) reticle-only monogram, (d) wordmark-only. Show with-orange-dot and without.

4. **Identity sheet.** One page: palette swatches with hex labels, type pairing samples (display headline + body + monospaced data), reticle motif at three sizes, the SCOUT REPORT eyebrow stamp, two short voice samples in their proper type.

**Don't:** use broadsheet serif; use multiple accents; let orange dominate; reference promo tropes (free bets, hot picks, bonuses). Anti-tipster, anti-gambling-promo.

**Do:** treat every page as an editorial publication that happens to deal with markets. Generous whitespace. One flame-orange moment per composition. Monospaced data should feel like a tactical briefing.

## Notes for next iteration

- If the navy reads too "broadcast TV" once on a real masthead, swap to `#112038` (deeper, almost obsidian) or `#14264A` (slightly bluer). Both stay clear of Sky Sports / Pinnacle territory.
- Direction was reached after rejecting three earlier directions: Floodlight (dark mode + sodium amber — recommended in landscape.pdf but dialed back at the operator's preference for clean white + bold orange after seeing Coaches' Voice as a reference); Broadsheet (cream + ink + oxblood); Pitch (cream + moss + signal-orange).
- The flame-orange is the single non-negotiable. The navy is the support pick that lets the orange punch without leaning gambling-promo (saturated yellow) or broadcast-TV (Sky red).
- Cluster-map context: this lands in the "publication, slightly bold" quadrant — only Coaches' Voice currently sits there, and it's pitched at coaches not fans, so it's a viable claim for Prophet.
