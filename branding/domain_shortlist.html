Decision frame

The economic question is misleading. $180 vs $10 isn't really 18× more expensive — it's a directional brand commitment vs a defensive lock. Cheap .com domains are insurance you can register all of and only use one. The .ai shortlist is a live commitment to a wordmark — only worth $180 each if the name actually becomes the brand.

Suggested move. Lock themorningxi.com + oddsprimer.com for ~$20 total as defensive insurance regardless of where this lands. Then pick one .ai if and only if a name on that list is the wordmark you commit to. Strongest .ai candidates: thescoutroom.ai (if "The Scout" is the lock-in name and we keep mocking up the scout-report system) or thestand.ai (if the brand needs maximum editorial flexibility and easier multi-sport extension).

Open question for Faktor. Of the seven names — The Scout · Scout Room · Odds Primer · Scoutline · The Pitch Line · The Stand · The Morning XI — which one feels like a publication you'd want to read on a Saturday morning? That's the lock-in.
