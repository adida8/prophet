Why this brand. Scouting culture in football is its own register — observational, evaluative, written down in notebooks. The Scout positions us as the fan with the better eye, not the gambler with the better tip. Reticle motif + scout-report stamp give the visual identity a recognisable structure across every piece of content. Caveat: the football "scout" space is crowded — Scouted, FM Scout, Scout.com legacy — and the brand would have to fight for SEO oxygen against existing publications.
