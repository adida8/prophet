Crowded zones:
  • Top-right (bold + product) — every gambling promo (bet365, William Hill, Paddy, Action Network) and every "loud" sports news portal (Goal, Sky, Sofascore-leaning, Oddschecker). The least useful place to land.
  • Bottom-right (muted + product) — Pinnacle, OddsPortal, FotMob, Polymarket. Clinical SaaS-grade tooling. Owns "trustworthy data utility." Hard to compete; not the brand's wedge anyway.
  • Bottom-left (muted + publication) — The Athletic, Mundial, Set Pieces, Coaches' Voice. The B&W editorial corner. Premium but requires decades of credibility to land here on day one.
Where the white space is:
  • Mid-left (publication, slightly bold) — only The Blizzard sits here (cream + hot pink) and Scouted FC (red on white). Distinctive, not gambling, not commodity score-tracker.
  • Dark-mode editorial — completely unowned. Every football publication defaults to white-paper. The dark-mode sites in the sample are all gambling (bet365, Action Network) — owning dark-mode-as-publication is genuinely empty quadrant.
  • Warm/muted but not B&W — cream-paper + a non-default accent (anything that isn't red/yellow/Sky-navy) is uncontested.

3 · Three palette directions

Each of these earns its rationale from the field above. None of them are safe defaults — each one chooses a piece of unowned territory. The recommendation is below; if the chosen one does not feel inevitable on second viewing, the other two are real fallbacks rather than consolation prizes.

A · Broadsheet
#F4EFE2 · #15171C · #7A1E26 · #9B927C · #FFFFFF
Warm chalk-cream, ink black, single oxblood accent. Reads as a Sunday-broadsheet football section. Earns its place by sitting next to The Athletic / Set Pieces but using oxblood (not BBC red, not Athletic red, not Scouted red) — a deeper, ink-printed shade. Risk: in five seconds, casual fans may not differentiate it from existing prestige editorial.

Recommendation: ship Direction B — "Floodlight." The sampled field shows that every football publication defaults to white-paper editorial, and every dark-mode site in the category is a gambling promo. A dark-mode editorial brand is the only quadrant where the visual register itself communicates "publication, not bookmaker" before a reader has parsed a single word. Sodium-amber is football-native (floodlight, captain's-armband, late-evening kickoff) and lands as a distinctive accent that no peer owns. The reticle/scope motif gains contrast and gravity against ink. Strongest single reason: in a field where category-shift requires earning trust on day one, picking the unowned quadrant of the cluster map is the cheapest way to be remembered. Strongest second reason: dark mode dramatizes the scout-report stamp far better than cream-paper does — every "scout report" headline reads as a briefing in a coach's office, not a Sunday-broadsheet column.
