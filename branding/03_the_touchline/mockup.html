Why this brand. "The Touchline" reads as a publication first, brand second — broadsheet typography, editorial italic masthead, grass-and-brass palette nods to a stadium without screaming football. Tone is closer to a Saturday supplement column than a betting feed, which is exactly the educational position we want. Major caveat: a competing football media brand The Touchline already operates with ~1.5M followers on X (@TouchlineX, thetouchline.co.uk), plus a separate newsletter at thetouchline.co. Brand collision risk is real and the .com / strong domain situation is poor. This brand wears beautifully but you'd be sharing a name with a much bigger incumbent in the same vertical.
