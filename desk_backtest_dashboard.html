Reading these numbers

Mean Brier 1.080. A perfectly calibrated forecast that always lands on the actual outcome would score 0; a coin flip on a 1X2 market scores ~0.667. Numbers above 1.0 indicate the model was confidently wrong on several matches — which is exactly what we'd expect from a sample of upsets.

Pick hit rate 0/3. All three Picks landed on the favourite; all three matches went to draws or upsets. This is small-sample selection bias by design — these matches were chosen for being famously chaotic. The real backtest covers 147 matches with normal variance, where calibration (not hit rate) becomes the headline number.

What good looks like. When this dashboard renders the full backtest:

  • Mean Brier in the 0.20–0.25 range, beating closing-market Brier by 1–2%
  • Reliability diagram bins clustered around the diagonal with calibration error under ±5pp per bin
  • Pick mix tilted toward Pass (most matches don't have an edge), with ~10–15% Pick rate
  • Pick hit rate roughly matching mean Pick probability — sanity check, not headline
