Two Ways We Get Paid

Tier A — License the Robot

$5K–$50K / month / customer

We sell our robot as software. The customer plugs it into their own system and runs it themselves. We never touch their money.

Like selling Shopify to shopkeepers — easy, safe, smaller paychecks.

Tier B — Run the Robot

$20K–$200K / month + share of profits

The customer hires us to operate the robot on their behalf. They provide the money to trade with; we keep a fee plus a share of what the robot earns.

Where the real money lives. Bigger paychecks, bigger responsibility.

The plan: start with Tier A (fast to sell, low risk). Once a customer trusts us, upsell them to Tier B. Same robot, same customer — just a bigger contract.

Who Buys From Us

  • Sportsbooks launching prediction-market sections — DraftKings, FanDuel, Fanatics. They already have millions of customers ready to trade.
  • New prediction-market startups — they need liquidity to launch. Our robot makes their market work from day one.
  • Professional trading firms — prop shops and quant funds who trade prediction markets as a business.

Rule: we only sell to customers who already bring their own shoppers. Our robot can't fill empty stalls in a ghost town.
