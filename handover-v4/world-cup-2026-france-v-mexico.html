Why the gap

France goes into the group as one of the strongest squads at the tournament. The market knows that. What the market is slow to fully price is the shape of the run-in: Mexico's selection picture has been unsettled, the venue suits France's structured tempo more than its set-piece anxieties, and the model reads the squad spine the same way it reads results — France first, comfortably.

A four-point gap is not enormous. It is enough to clear our bar. We treat anything inside that bar as a Pass — even when we like the side editorially.

What this verdict is, and isn't

A Pick is a read on the price, not on the result. It says the market is offering France at a number our model considers light. Whether France actually wins is a separate question, decided on the grass. We publish the gap; what readers do with it is their decision, made elsewhere.
