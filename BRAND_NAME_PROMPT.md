# Prompt for Gemini / ChatGPT — brand name suggestions (football-leaning)

*Paste everything below the divider. Works in both tools as-is.*

---

I'm naming a new product. I'd like you to suggest brand names that fit the brief tightly. Read the whole brief before suggesting anything.

## What we're building

A **football-first, educational guide to prediction markets**. The product compares prices for the same football event across multiple prediction-market venues (Kalshi, Polymarket, and others), highlights the best price, and explains in plain English what each market is actually asking and what the price means.

The MVP launches with the 2026 FIFA World Cup as the opening act. Football is the long-term home — Premier League, Champions League, big tournaments, transfer windows, manager moves. Other sports may follow eventually, but football is the anchor and the brand should feel football-native.

We are NOT a sportsbook. We are NOT a trading terminal. We are NOT a tipster. We're closer to "The Athletic for prediction-market questions" — opinionated, editorial, football-fluent, trustworthy.

## Audience

A football fan (casual to engaged) who's curious about prediction markets but doesn't really know how they work. They watch matches, check odds before kickoff, argue tactics with friends — but YES/NO contracts and implied probability are new concepts to them. The brand needs to sound at home in their football-media diet.

Secondary: existing prediction-market users who want a faster way to compare venues for football markets.

## Brand personality

- Clear, honest, calm, modern, slightly nerdy in a friendly way.
- Football-fluent — uses football vocabulary the way a tactics writer or a smart pundit would, not the way a sportsbook ad would.
- "The smart friend who explains things at the bar after the match" — not the Wall Street trader, not the casino host, not the tipster, not the crypto degen.
- Editorial / publication vibe with football culture in its bones.

## Calibration — names that get the vibe right (do NOT suggest these, just for tone-setting)

**Football media that nails the editorial-credible football voice:** The Athletic. Tifo Football. The Anfield Wrap. Men in Blazers. These Football Times. 90min. OneFootball. Football Cliches (the podcast). The Coaches' Voice.

**Non-football editorial brands with the same intellectual posture:** Wirecutter. Stratechery. FiveThirtyEight. Stat Significant.

Notice the pattern: confident, knowledgeable, "knows its football" or "knows its data" — none of these sound like a sportsbook, a hedge fund, or a fan account.

## Football vocabulary is encouraged — but be smart about it

We WANT football-rooted names that feel native to the game. Tactical terms, fan rituals, match-day language, football history — all fair game if used well.

**Smart football vocabulary worth exploring:** half-space, set piece, build-up, transition, press, false nine, nutmeg, channel, third, line, dummy, gegenpress, catenaccio, total football, the table, form guide, fixture, matchday, kickoff, full time, stoppage time, injury time, derby, cup tie, replay, gaffer, terrace, kop, badge, kit, fixture list.

**Lazy football clichés to AVOID:** kick, goal, net, pitch, header, striker, score, win, victor, champ. These are the words sportsbooks have already burned out — they signal "betting app" not "smart football publication."

## Other things to AVOID

- Anything ending in `-bet`, `-book`, `-wager`, `-stakes`, `-picks`, `-tips`, `-odds` (sportsbook signal — yes, even though "odds" is in our category description).
- Anything with `edge`, `alpha`, `pro`, `signal`, `quant` (trader-bro signal).
- Anything with `AI` jammed in (commodity feel — half the products in this space have "AI" in the name).
- Crypto / finance bingo: gem, moon, rocket, lambo, ape, hodl, vault, capital.
- **Existing names in the prediction-market space (do NOT suggest these or near-variants):** Polymarket, Kalshi, OddsJam, Oddpool, PredictionEdge / yourpredictionedge, Prediction Market Tools, Manifold, FORS, PolyRouter, Smarkets, Betfair Exchange.
- **Football-media collisions to screen against:** The Athletic, Tifo Football, The Anfield Wrap, Men in Blazers, These Football Times, 90min, OneFootball, 433, Goal, FotMob, Sofascore, Transfermarkt, WhoScored.
- **Sportsbook collisions to screen against:** DraftKings, FanDuel, Stake, Bet365, William Hill.

## Constraints

- English-language brand (international audience, English-speaking markets first).
- Pronounceable on first try.
- Short — ideally 1 to 3 syllables, 2 words maximum.
- Googleable — no overly common dictionary word that gets buried in unrelated search results.
- `.com` availability is strongly preferred. Flag whether the .com appears to be available based on what you can verify, and acknowledge uncertainty where it exists.
- Football-native, but should age into other sports if we expand later. Avoid names so World-Cup-specific they're stale by August 2026.
- Friendly to a wordmark logo (no awkward letter combinations or accents).

## What to return — exact format

Suggest **15 names organised into 3 football-leaning buckets of 5**:

- **Bucket 1 — Tactical / analytical football** (names drawn from how football is played, analysed, or coached — set pieces, formations, tactical concepts, modern football vocabulary).
- **Bucket 2 — Cultural / matchday football** (names drawn from fan culture, matchday rituals, terraces, history, the lived experience of being a football fan).
- **Bucket 3 — Editorial / smart-friend** (names that don't have to be football vocabulary but signal the Wirecutter-for-PMs / Athletic-for-prediction-markets editorial voice — and would feel at home in football media).

For each name, give me:

1. **The name** (exactly as styled — capitalisation matters).
2. **One-line meaning / etymology** — where it comes from, what it evokes, what football reference (if any).
3. **Why it fits the brief** (2 sentences max — what this name signals about us).
4. **Domain check** — does the .com appear available? Flag uncertainty honestly. Suggest a backup TLD if .com is likely taken.
5. **Collision flag** — any obvious clash with companies in betting, sports media, prediction markets, or fintech that I should worry about.
6. **Tagline option** — one tagline matching the name, under 10 words.

After the 15, end with **your top 3 overall picks ranked 1–3**, each with one paragraph on why you'd choose them over the rest. At least one of your top 3 should be football-rooted.

## Final note

Don't try to be clever for cleverness' sake. I'd rather have a name that's slightly boring but absolutely right than a name that's witty but signals the wrong thing. Football-credible and editorial-credible beats viral-clever every time for this brand.
