France v Argentina: a 2018 final replayed at half the spread.

The market is splitting eight points across six venues for what looks, on paper, like a coin flip. Here's where the prices disagree, and what to read into it.

Odds Primer Editorial · Prices desk·6-min read·Refreshed 14:08 ET

It is the rerun of 2018's final, eight years on, and the prices want it to be a coin flip — but they don't agree by how much. Argentina opened a slight favorite at the closing whistle of qualifying; Kalshi has them at 52¢. DraftKings has them at +115 — implied 47%. The gap is small in dollars and large in meaning.

Three things are doing the work in the price: the venue (Mexico City, in the afternoon, at 2,240m); the bench (Mbappé still listed for France, Messi still listed for Argentina, both with quiet question marks attached); and a market memory of penalties and the crooked path that particular fixture took the last time it ended in extra time.

The cleanest read is that Kalshi at 52¢ is doing what prediction markets do well — pricing a near-toss at the population's actual estimate, net of fees. The 8-point spread to BetMGM is half storyline (Mbappé) and half venue character: BetMGM has been the slowest of the six to absorb injury news this tournament.

None of this is a tip. The spread is the story; we don't have a seventh price to add.
