Thumbnail · for feed-first contexts

In a Twitter / Threads / LinkedIn feed, the OG card renders at roughly 240px wide. At that size, the dossier table goes to texture — readers can't parse rows, they just see a grid. The thumbnail variant trades the table for typography: giant match line, giant price, one spread fact. Same 1200×630 canvas, different visual hierarchy.
