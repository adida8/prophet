Information we collect

We collect three categories of information.

Information you give us

When you sign up for our newsletter, create an account, or contact us, we collect the data you provide — typically an email address, and any message content you send.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Service we collect standard log data: IP address, device and browser type, referring page, pages viewed, and timestamps. We use cookies and similar technologies for session continuity, preference storage, and aggregate analytics. See section 06.

Information from third parties

If you arrive from, or click through to, a partner venue (for example, a prediction-market operator), we may receive limited referral metadata. We do not receive your account information, balances, or trading activity from those venues.

How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • send you the newsletter or other communications you have requested, and let you opt out at any time;
  • understand which content is read and which is not, in aggregate, so we can prioritise editorial work;
  • detect, prevent, and address abuse, fraud, or technical issues;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers for advertising targeting.

Legal bases for processing

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on:

  • Consent — for marketing emails and non-essential cookies.
  • Legitimate interests — for security, fraud prevention, aggregate analytics, and improving the Service.
  • Contract — to provide a service you have requested.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to retain or disclose data.

Sharing and disclosure

We share information only with: service providers who process data on our behalf (hosting, email delivery, analytics) under written contracts that limit their use of the data; authorities, when required by law or to protect rights and safety; and any successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

When you click a venue handoff link (“View source on [venue]”), you leave the Service and the destination's privacy practices apply. We do not transmit your personal data to the venue beyond what your browser sends in a normal outbound link (referrer header).

Cookies and similar technologies

We use a small number of cookies. Strictly necessary cookies keep the Service functional. Preference cookies remember your settings. Analytics cookies help us understand aggregate usage. You can refuse non-essential cookies at the consent banner or in your browser settings; refusing analytics cookies will not break the Service.
