Locked design decisions

  • Layout A — compact dual-chip. Two venue chips per card, side by side. Same skeleton across all six variants; mobile stacks the chips.
  • No unified "Market" line. Each venue chip carries its own implied probability — the price IS the venue's implied probability (52¢ on a binary contract = 52%). Model probability stays as one number above the chips ("Model 56% — The Desk's estimate, before venue prices"). Per-chip edge is shown on each chip ("+4pp on Polymarket, +3pp on Kalshi"), making the venue spread legible at a glance.
  • Venue CTA is a solid ink button — "Open Polymarket ↗" / "Open Kalshi ↗". 44px tap target on mobile, full chip width, flame arrow. Words stay editorial; visual weight does the conversion push (L3 treatment). No anti-tipster rule breakage.
  • Price format = cents primary, American odds secondary — "52¢" on the top line, "−108" on the line below. Cents read directly as probability — reinforces the Learn page's "price = probability" framing for prediction-market natives, while American odds serve sportsbook-trained readers.
  • Numbers compute. Cents and American odds on every chip are internally consistent (52¢ ↔ −108, 16¢ ↔ +525, etc.). Edge = Model% − Cents per venue. Edge is per-chip, so the different prices give different edges visibly.
  • Match vs Outright shares the same component. The two deltas are the cv-when subtitle (kickoff time vs resolution date) and the cv-title (paired teams vs single team).
  • Flame is reserved for Pick signals. Pass uses graphite-soft; Avoid uses ink-bar + flame strikethrough on cents. Negative edges render in ink, not flame.
  • "Read the market case →" stays as the editorial overflow CTA. Internal traffic (the dossier). The two Open-venue buttons are the affiliate path. Two surfaces per card.

Contract implication

  • Each card now needs two venue price points, not one. Current contract surfaces a single verdict.market_venue + verdict.price ("best" venue).
  • For the dual-chip card, The Desk needs to publish market_prices: { polymarket: {price, implied_p, source_url}, kalshi: {price, implied_p, source_url} } per market — both venues, always.
