Polymarket has France at 16¢, an implied probability† of 16%. Kalshi has them at 14¢. The gap is small in the abstract, large for a market this liquid‡.

†Implied probability. On a "yes" contract priced from 0 to $1, the price is the implied probability. 16¢ ≈ 16%. American odds (e.g. +550) need a quick conversion — see the glossary.
‡Liquid. A market is liquid when there's enough volume that one trade doesn't move the price meaningfully.
