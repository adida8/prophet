# WC 2026 edition — strategic brief

**Status:** locked framing · 2026-05-06
**Decided after:** six rounds of rejected logo modifiers (quiet attachments, banner attachments, FIFA-style "26" treatments) → diagnostic that the whole approach was wrong → ChatGPT review confirmed the reframe.

---

## The frame

> **Odds Primer is the masthead; World Cup 2026 is the issue.**

Not a sub-brand. Not a campaign skin. Not a sectional masthead. Not an event mark. **An editorial franchise.**

The locked logo carries the publication. The WC edition is expressed through **layout hierarchy** — recurring strips, rules, datelines, issue labels, match cards, chart-led covers — not through symbol-making.

The cover does the work. The brand stays the brand.

## Why this won

- A **sub-brand** would create identity overhead and pull design toward FIFA-adjacent territory. Wrong register.
- A **sectional masthead** still treats the WC as a logo problem. Still wrong.
- An **event mark** is what FIFA already owns. Competing with FIFA's identity language is unwinnable; borrowing it makes us derivative.
- Treating the WC like **a magazine issue** plays to our strengths: we already commit to editorial register, we already have a chart-led visual system, we already write headlines.

## Treatment specs — top 3 surfaces

### 1. Homepage / WC hub hero — broadsheet front page

Locked logo top-left (or centered depending on layout). Thin editorial rule beneath. Then a small mono line:

> `WORLD CUP 2026 · EDITION 01 · MATCH MARKETS EXPLAINED`

The hero is **not** a trophy, ball, stadium, or flag. The hero is a **large chart-table hybrid**: four or six venue columns, one featured question, one flame-underlined best price, one big serif headline.

Example rhythm:

> **Headline (Source Serif):** *The same World Cup question has six different prices.*
> **Deck (Source Serif body):** We compare the venues, translate the odds, and show where the market disagrees.

Type stack: Source Serif for the headline, JetBrains Mono for prices and dates, Inter Tight for labels. **Flame appears once** — underline the key disagreement number or the best-price marker. Locked logo stays untouched and authoritative, like the masthead.

### 2. OG / social cards — magazine cover

Cover, not app screenshot. Oversized serif headline, one tabular-number fact, compact Odds Primer stamp. **No photography. No FIFA trophy. No fake tournament badge.**

Structure:
- **Top-left:** locked logo (or stacked stamp variant).
- **Top-right:** edition label `WORLD CUP 2026`.
- **Center:** one provocative editorial line.
- **Bottom:** a single data strip.

Example:

> **Headline:** *France are priced like favorites in one market — outsiders in another.*
> **Bottom strip:** `FRA TO WIN · 32¢ · 29% · 41% · 11PT SPREAD`
> Flame only under `11PT SPREAD` or under the best venue.

WC energy comes from **editorial tension**, not decoration.

### 3. In-article match / market callouts — match dossier module

Repeatable module. The edition becomes ownable here.

- **Top row:** `FRA v ARG · Jun 12 · Group Stage`
- **Disciplined table:** venue · price · implied probability · note.
- **Best price:** bold navy + flame underline + small `best` tag.
- **Left rail:** small vertical edition tag — `WC 2026 / MARKET BRIEF`.

Logo does not need to appear inside every module. The design language — rules, mono numerics, cream paper, flame underline — does the brand work.

## References to pattern-match

| Reference | Why |
|---|---|
| **NYT Magazine World Cup Issue** (Gail Bichler / Raul Aguila) | A sports event treated through editorial cover logic, not event-brand imitation. |
| **The Atlantic 2019 redesign** | Classical editorial authority, strong nameplate discipline, restrained hierarchy over decorative theming. |
| **France Football redesign** (République Studio) | Football-native, premium, typographic, magazine-first rather than sportsbook-first. |
| **The Athletic's special editorial packages** (draft guides, longform sports explainers) | Productized editorial rhythm. Pattern-match the structure; keep visually calmer than them. |

## Anti-patterns — explicit

Do **not** use any of:

- The trophy, an official-ish "26," host-country flag palette, soccer-ball icons, pitch diagrams as decoration, confetti, badges, crests, national flags as UI chrome, or anything resembling FIFA's event identity.
- "World Cup Edition" baked into the logo lockup.
- A secondary mark.
- Green for "best." Flame as urgency.
- CTAs like "take advantage," "bet now," "lock in," "cash."
- Live match states styled like sportsbook tickers.

## What the edition should feel like

A serious football issue about **price disagreement**. Not a World Cup fan site.
