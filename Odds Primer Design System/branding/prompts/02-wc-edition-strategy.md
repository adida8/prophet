## The strategic frame

I've been trying to make the logo do "World Cup edition" work — adding banners, sashes, dagger marks, FIFA-style "26" digit treatments. Six rounds of options, all rejected. The diagnosis I landed on is:

> **FIFA brands the event. We cover the event. Those are different design jobs.**

When you brand the event, the logo *is* the World Cup mark — see FIFA's "26" with the trophy. When you cover the event, the logo stays the publication, and the *cover* does the editorial work. Time magazine doesn't change its logo for Person of the Year. The Atlantic doesn't restyle its masthead for an anniversary issue. The cover changes; the brand stays.

Our locked design system already says this: **"Editioned, not skinned. A masthead strip and tournament-flavored section dividers, but the brand stays the brand."**
