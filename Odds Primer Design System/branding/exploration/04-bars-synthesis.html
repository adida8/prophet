07

Risks & mitigations

Each top-3 risk from the review, with how this synthesis addresses it.

A2 risk
"Caret may read as a stray UI annotation."
Mitigated by using the same caret throughout the product as the "best price" pointer (above price chips, above outright-winner bars). The mark is a miniature of a recurring system gesture, not a one-off accent.
A5 risk
"Mono row may become precious or illegible."
Mitigated by demoting the mono row to the editorial lockup only (about, footer, longform). Primary lockup has no mono row. It's a footnote variant, never the headline.
A6 risk
"Stacked may feel too institutional / not enough product."
Mitigated by using stacked only on the social card and venue handoff card. Masthead, navigation, and app chrome stay inline — keeps the brand's product-leaning balance at the 60-position.
