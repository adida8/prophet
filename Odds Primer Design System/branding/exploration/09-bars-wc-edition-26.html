The "26" treatment — translating the FIFA energy

FIFA's 2026 mark sets a giant "26" behind the gold trophy. The trophy is their hero asset; the digits frame it. For us, the bars glyph plays the trophy's role — our hero. Big "26" framing the bars, "Odds Primer" anchored below.

Reference
FIFA's hero treatment is massive "26" digits + trophy nested between + FIFA wordmark below. The trophy partly eclipses the digits — it sits in front.
Translation
Same construction in our system: massive "26" + bars glyph nested between + "Odds Primer" wordmark below. The bars sit in front of the digits — flame third bar reads against the "2" or the "6" depending on negative space.

05

What this gets us — and what to watch

Strengths
The bars stop being a small mark and start being the tournament hero.

The "26" is a direct reference to FIFA's hero treatment — borrowed authority on a launch surface, but expressed in our own visual language.

Construction scales cleanly: hero (280px digit) → social card (~200px) → masthead (36px). The bars-between-digits relationship survives.
Watchouts
Trademark. "26" alone is not FIFA's mark, but the construction (digit + central asset + below-wordmark) is FIFA's. Worth a quick legal sense-check before launch.

Editioned, not skinned. Reserve this treatment for WC-specific surfaces (hub, OG cards, special-issue articles). The default brand stays the locked v2 lockup.

Color discipline. V3 (flame everywhere) is loud but flame loses its "best price" meaning when it's used decoratively at scale. Keep flame on the third bar wherever it appears.
