04

Separate question — venue link copy

Not a logo question, but flagged in the same review. The design system locks "Open on [venue] ↗" as the venue handoff phrase. Review proposes "View source on [venue]" or "Compare on [venue]" as further from the transaction.

Decide separately

Should we keep "Open on Kalshi" or move to "View source on Kalshi" / "Compare on Kalshi"?

This touches the design system's locked rules, not the logo. "Open on" is neutral but does direct the user to the venue. "View source" reframes the venue as a citation, not a destination — fits the patient-teacher voice. "Compare on" only makes sense if the user is going to the venue to verify our comparison, which they're not. Recommendation: revisit "Open on" → "View source on" as a system-wide change, but treat as separate from the logo lock.
