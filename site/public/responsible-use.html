Responsible use · 3 min read

Prediction markets involve risk

Prediction-market prices move quickly. Outcomes are uncertain. You can lose money.

Only use third-party venues if you understand the risks, meet the venue’s eligibility rules, and are legally allowed to participate in your location.

Our content is not advice

Odds Primer content is for informational and editorial purposes only.

A Pick is not an instruction.
A Pass is not a prohibition.
An Avoid is not a guarantee that a market will perform badly.

Each verdict is a market read, not personal advice.

Before acting elsewhere

Before using any third-party venue, check:

  • The live market price
  • The venue’s rules
  • The market resolution criteria
  • Fees
  • Liquidity
  • Local laws
  • Your own risk tolerance

If markets stop being fun

Do not use prediction markets if they create financial stress, emotional pressure, or compulsive behavior.

Take breaks. Set limits. Do not chase losses.

If you feel you may have a gambling or trading problem, seek support from a qualified professional or a responsible gambling support organization in your country.
