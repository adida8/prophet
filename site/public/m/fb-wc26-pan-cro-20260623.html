Three-way split from the model: Panama to win at 13%, the draw at 12%, and Croatia to win at 75%. The Desk publishes a three-way read against the same W/D/L resolution Polymarket and Kalshi settle on — not a scoreline guess. Croatia sits at 75% of the mass.

On the same three-way, Polymarket: Panama to win at 12%, the draw at 22%, and Croatia to win at 62%. The Croatia side trades at 62% on Polymarket, which prices out at American odds of −167. That's where the engine differs: our number reads 75%, +12.2pp wider.

Bootstrap discipline matters most on the longshots — wide bands on small point estimates collapse the Pick rate fast. On Croatia the lower bound is 66%, which keeps the lower-bound gap at +3.9pp against the market — past +3pp, so the Pick clears. The claim is narrow: on this fixture, on this side, the price is below what the underlying read supports. What would close this gap: confirmed team news that pulls the prior down on the side we like, a venue or weather change, or a tournament suspension landing before kickoff.
